01 / Research interests

From physical insight to intelligent models.

A focus on renewable-rich power systems, with experience in impedance modeling and machine learning.

Modeling · Control · Analysis

Making sense of a changing grid.

As renewable generation grows, understanding the interactions between devices and the grid becomes increasingly important. I am interested in models and analysis methods that help explain these dynamics and support power-system control.

Related research experience↗

Machine learning · Domain knowledge

Learning from data, grounded in physics.

My previous work uses support vector machines to predict equivalent-circuit parameters across operating points. Building on this experience, I am interested in broader applications of AI to power-system modeling and analysis.

Read selected publications↗

Impedance · Admittance · Model reduction

Models that remain understandable.

My research experience includes frequency-domain modeling of permanent-magnet synchronous generators (PMSGs), reduced equivalent circuits, and impedance participation factors for investigating oscillations and weakly damped components.
